Do not use AutoEdit to make or share:

  • anything that sexualises or harms a child, ever (we report it to the authorities);
  • sexual content of a person without their consent, or any "deepfake" that puts a real person in a sexual, humiliating or false situation;
  • someone else's work (videos, music, pictures, fonts) you have no right to use;
  • a private person's face, voice or private details without their consent, or anything meant to stalk, bully or harass someone;
  • threats, incitement to violence, terrorism, or hate against people for who they are;
  • scams, fake news meant to deceive, election disinformation, or pretending to be someone else;
  • anything else illegal in the Philippines or where you are.

Also do not: try to get around limits or the one-trial rule, share accounts to resell the service, upload files meant to attack the service (malware, files built to crash software), probe or overload it, or use it to send spam.

See something that breaks these rules? Report it. A person reviews every report. We may hold or delete videos, suspend or close accounts, and report to the authorities when the law requires.
