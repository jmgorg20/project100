AutoEdit uses AI to edit: speech-to-text makes the captions, and a language model plans the cuts, captions, effects and graphics from your instruction. A person (you) decides what to post.

  • What the AI does: transcribes, picks the best parts, removes pauses, writes captions, chooses effects and music cues. It does not create fake people or put words in anyone's mouth.
  • What it sees: the audio of your video (for the transcript), then the transcript, your instruction and facts about the video (length, size). It is sent to the AI providers named in the Privacy notice only to make your edit, never to train models.
  • Mistakes: AI can mishear words, especially names, Taglish and slang. Check captions before posting; you can fix them in the app.
  • Labels: every result is marked "Edited with AI" in the app. If your video shows realistic people, places or events that were made or changed by AI, use the platform's AI label when you post (YouTube "altered or synthetic content", TikTok "AI-generated", Meta "AI info").
  • Models: the service does not include or download AI model weights; it uses the AI providers' services.

Sa madaling salita (Taglish)

AI ang gumagawa ng captions at nagpaplano ng edit mula sa instruction mo; ikaw pa rin ang nagdedesisyon kung ano ang ipo-post. Hindi ito gumagawa ng pekeng tao. Puwedeng magkamali sa salita (lalo na pangalan at Taglish): tingnan muna. Kung may makatotohanang bagay na gawa o binago ng AI, i-on ang AI label ng platform.
